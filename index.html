ABOUT

Studio Grail was born out of a dream to create hauntingly beautiful games with serious themes. Founded in 2023 and based in Helsinki Finland, we're an independent studio dedicated to crafting immersive narrative experiences that challenge perceptions and explore the darker side of the human psyche. The  studio is led by sole developer Josianne, but makes use of a strong support network and selective outsourcing in making its games a reality.
